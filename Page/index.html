Batch billing

A skail function bills a batch of orders: it asks the city hall for one service invoice per order, in waves of 10, each invoice a child run with its own history. The simulated city hall is down on about 1 call in 10: skail retries only that invoice, about 15 seconds later, and the others go on. An authorized invoice sends the PDF to the customer; a rejected or pending one goes to finance, and the batch does not stop.
